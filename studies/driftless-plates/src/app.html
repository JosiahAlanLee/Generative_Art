Subject

Rock sets the outcrops, mesa faces and lake bluffs: sandstone towers, dolomite ledges with red cedar, or jointed quartzite over talus. The rock-top house always stands on sandstone.

Motif weights

Weights bias what Auto picks and how much of the page each motif gets. They blend in one scene rather than switching modes.

Hand

Imperfection adds wobble, ink skips, ruled-line overshoot and pen misregistration.

Palette

Strength

Lower strength mixes every ink toward the paper colour, for plates that sit behind text.

Orientation

The A4 plot master keeps every stroke inside the extent the Codex plate validator checks: 8 mm from each side, 10 mm from the top and 9 mm from the foot. It takes no bleed and no colour offset.

Text area

Position
Treatment

Text block is the Codex frame shared by the Clavis, De Vinculis and both parables: 52–452 pt across, from 82 pt below the top to 55 pt above the foot of the 7 × 10 in page. Other trims scale it; a spread gets one block per page. Fade out thins the linework to a clearing for type. Lighten keeps the drawing but moves it into the tint ink.

Files

SVG is millimetre-true with one Inkscape layer per pen, ready for AxiDraw-type plotters or vpype. PNG renders at 300 dpi with the resolution stored in the file.
