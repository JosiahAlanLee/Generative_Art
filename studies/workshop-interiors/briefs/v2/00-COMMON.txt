WORKSHOP INTERIORS / BRIEFS, SECOND PASS / COMMON TERMS
Codex Intervallorum. Stroke-only plates built from Blender geometry.
Replaces the diffusion-era wording for these four scenes. 21 October 2026.

These briefs are written for the medium the plates are now made in: evaluated
geometry, hidden-line visibility, two inks, no washes, no tone except hatching.
"Copperplate", "washes", "slate-blue insets" and the other diffusion vocabulary
are withdrawn. Where a brief below contradicts the recorded original brief, this
one governs the vector plate; the original stays in the record as the source of
the scene.

THE SERIES' RULES THAT APPLY
1. Black records an arrangement. Red marks an intervention or a question
   (Matricis, "Reading the hands"). In Book III: black is the route offered,
   red is what happens to the incident ("Directions for descending").
2. A reserved blank contains no omitted answer. Reserved paper is a drawn
   decision, not an unfinished area; it is listed in each brief by name.
3. Every plate regenerates from its manifest. The scene.json, seed and
   constants are the plate.
4. Figures: Book VI's plates carry no figure by its own rule. Books I-III and
   VII have no such rule; each brief says what it does about hands.

INKS
Two: black and vermilion. The brass ink used in the first pass is withdrawn
from these four plates; tracery and instrument ornament are black at the
ornament weight below. If the author decides to keep brass as a third ink,
that is a series-wide decision and every plate in the series must re-declare
its ink count; the briefs below assume two.

Masters are engraved AT PRINTED SIZE. No nominal 180 mm master is scaled down.
All line weights below are millimetres on the printed page.

LINE WEIGHTS, PRINT EDITION (offset or 600 dpi and better)
  hatch line                 0.10
  minimum gap, one family    0.22   (cross-hatch may take a pocket to 0.80 cover)
  crease edge                0.16   x depth factor 0.80 (far) to 1.30 (near)
  silhouette edge            0.25   x same depth factor
  backdrop edge (walls, bookcase backing, floor joints)   0.12
  ornament / tracery / book spines, black                 0.12
  strands, cords, threads, black                          0.14
  vermilion route or thread  0.32   vermilion terminal / dot   0.40
  outline of a reserved region                            0.16
  The vermilion layer is the heaviest continuous line on every plate.

LINE WEIGHTS, PLOT MASTERS (A4, 0.3 mm fineliner; a separate parameter set)
  hatch: pen width; gap 0.65 mm; darkest pocket about 0.65 cover by crosshatch
  silhouettes: double pass offset 0.15 mm; creases: single pass
  vermilion: 0.5 mm pen, or double pass of 0.3
  tracery: half the cell count of the print edition
A plot master is a different drawing with the same geometry. It is not the print
file scaled. Until a plot master has been cut, the caveat "no physical plot test"
stays on the print edition's colophon.

TONE PLAN
Each brief names its zones with target hatch coverage (fraction of area inked,
measured in 4 mm cells at printed size). The plan is a hierarchy, not a palette:
the eye must land on the first-named zone first. The darkest zone on every plate
is a shadow pocket next to the subject, not the subject.

WHAT EVERY PLATE MUST DELIVER
- A proof at printed size on the Codex page with its caption, rasterised at
  600 dpi binary, beside the previous selection. That proof is the review
  object; the 180 mm contact sheet is not.
- The legibility and hierarchy checks in RENDERING-NOTES.md in addition to the
  existing geometry checks. "Delivered" is not "visible".
- The manifest's limits paragraph, unchanged in substance.
