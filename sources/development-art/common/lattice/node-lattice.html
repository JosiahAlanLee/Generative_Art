How the plate is made

A circle packing grown inside the host becomes a power diagram. Deep cells stretch along the drape and become panes between curved ribs: chains of round cells, slate panes with veins, lace, dark hollows. A band of small cells at the edge becomes an open net. Past the silhouette the diagram's edges become tendrils that drift outward and down, ending in terminal dots, and drips fall from the undersides.

Traced hosts take only the outline of a render; everything inside is regrown. Native hosts use no render at all. The plot SVG is an A4 figure master in millimetres: strokes only, dots as coils, tone as hatching. Ink and red gives the books' two layers and two inks; four pens gives one layer per pen, light to dark.
